# Popoyo Beauty Website Specification

## Purpose

Build a simple luxury website for **Popoyo Beauty**, an in-home spa service in the Popoyo area of Nicaragua.

The site should make it easy for travelers, villa guests, retreat hosts, wedding guests, surf groups, couples, and families to request premium beauty and wellness services at their accommodation.

The website must feel calm, elevated, tropical, trustworthy, and easy to use on mobile.

Do not generate a complex app. This should be a polished landing page with a lightweight booking request system.

## Brand

1. Luxury spa care brought to your villa

Recommended primary tagline:

**Luxury spa care brought to your villa**

### Brand positioning

Popoyo Beauty is not a cheap local salon and not a high-end resort spa. It sits in the middle as a premium mobile convenience service.

The offer is:

- More elevated and organized than finding a random local provider
- More accessible than resort spa pricing
- Easier than driving to a spa or coordinating many providers
- Designed for tourists, families, retreat guests, weddings, surf travelers, and villa stays
- Delivered directly to the client’s villa, hotel, Airbnb, retreat center, or beach house

## Market and pricing assumptions

Pricing should be in **USD**.

Market logic:

- Nicaragua local beauty and massage pricing can be low.
- Popoyo area tourism has premium demand because of villas, surf tourism, retreats, weddings, and resort guests.
- Nearby resort spa pricing can be much higher than independent providers.
- Popoyo Beauty should price as premium mobile convenience, below resort spa pricing but above casual local services.
- The price must leave room for provider pay, coordination, travel time, supplies, WhatsApp support, and business margin.

Recommended policy:

- 50% deposit to confirm
- Remaining balance due after service
- 24 to 48 hour cancellation policy
- Travel fee may apply outside central Popoyo, Guasacate, Playa Santana, Hacienda Iguana, Las Salinas, and nearby areas
- Minimum service total for in-home visit: $55
- Group bookings and retreats quoted manually

## Booking system requirements

Build a booking request system, not a full automated booking calendar.

The booking system should allow users to:

1. Select multiple services
2. View selected services
3. See estimated total
4. See estimated total duration
5. See estimated deposit
6. Enter booking details
7. Send request via WhatsApp

### Booking fields

Required fields:

- Name
- WhatsApp number
- Location or accommodation name
- Preferred date
- Preferred time
- Number of guests
- Selected services

Optional fields:

- Notes
- Pressure preference
- Allergies
- Pregnancy status
- Children’s ages
- Parking or access details
- Special occasion
- Need for English or Spanish-speaking provider
- Preferred payment method

### Booking calculations

The system should calculate:

- Total service price
- Total estimated duration
- 50% deposit
- Remaining balance

Formula:

- Total price = sum of selected service prices
- Deposit = 50% of total price
- Balance = total price minus deposit

Childcare logic:

- Base childcare = $24 for 2 hours
- Extra childcare = $12 per hour after 2 hours
- If childcare is selected, show a field for requested childcare hours
- Minimum childcare hours = 2

Travel fee logic:

Show travel fee as a note, not an automatic calculation for launch.

Suggested copy:

Travel may be included in central Popoyo and nearby areas. A small travel fee may apply for harder-to-reach villas, remote roads, or appointments outside the normal service area.

## FAQ section

Include these FAQs.

### Do you come to my villa or hotel?

Yes. Popoyo Beauty is an in-home service. We come to your villa, hotel, Airbnb, retreat center, or beach house.

### Can I book multiple services at once?

Yes. You can combine massage, nails, hair, body treatments, and childcare in one request.

### Is the appointment confirmed automatically?

No. Your request is confirmed by WhatsApp after we verify provider availability, travel time, and the final total.

### Do you offer childcare?

Yes, childcare may be available as an add-on while parents receive services. A parent or guardian must remain on the property.

### Do you serve groups or retreats?

Yes. Retreats, weddings, surf groups, and family villa days can be quoted manually.

### Do you bring everything?

Yes. The provider brings the core supplies needed for the service. For massage, confirm whether a massage table is included or whether the client needs space for setup.

### What should I prepare?

A clean, shaded, quiet area with enough room for the service. For massage, a space where a table can fit. For nails or hair, good light and a chair or table are helpful.

### Can I request a specific time?

Yes. You can request a preferred time. Final timing depends on provider availability and travel route.

### What if I am sunburned?

Tell us before booking. Some services may need to be adapted. For strong sunburn, deep pressure, exfoliation, or heat may not be appropriate.

### Do you offer prenatal massage?

Yes, if an appropriate provider is available. Please share your pregnancy stage and any medical considerations before confirmation.

## Important implementation notes for Claude

- Do not create a complicated backend unless specifically requested.
- Start with a polished front-end booking request flow.
- Multi-service selection is required.
- WhatsApp request generation is required.
- Pricing should be stored in an editable service list, not hardcoded across multiple places.
- Make service categories easy to update.
- Make WhatsApp number easy to update.
- Make payment methods easy to update.
- Make service area easy to update.
- Booking does not need to process payment at launch.
- Booking does not need real-time provider availability at launch.
- The appointment is only confirmed manually by WhatsApp.

- A visitor immediately understands Popoyo Beauty offers in-home spa services in Popoyo
- The site feels premium, calm, and trustworthy
- Services are clear and priced
- A user can select multiple services at once
- The selected services appear in a booking summary
- The site calculates estimated total, deposit, and duration

- The booking message includes all relevant details
- The site works well on mobile
- The business owner can easily update prices, services, WhatsApp number, and payment methods
